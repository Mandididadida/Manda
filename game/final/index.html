How to Play

Two players take turns fishing.

On each turn, click Reel In or tap a character. You may catch 1 fish, or you may miss.

After one player fishes, their turn ends. Then it becomes the other player's turn.

The first player to catch 5 fish wins!

Congratulations!

Congratulations! The winner caught 5 fish first and won the game!
